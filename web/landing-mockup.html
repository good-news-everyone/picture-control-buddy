From your edits

Show it a photo you edited. Get the look back for your camera.

You have a few favourite edits from Lightroom, Snapseed or anywhere else. Give the tool the NEF and the edited JPEG side by side, and it works out a picture control that makes the camera do the same thing.

  1. Drop a NEF and its edited JPEGOne pair works. Three or four different scenes work better.
  2. Press “Fit”About a minute on a laptop. Make a tea.
  3. Check, tweak, saveCompare with your JPEG, nudge a slider if you like, save the .NP3 file.

Pick a frame. Drag the line on the right to check the result against the edited JPEG.

By hand

Make your own picture control. Sliders, not science.

Open any NEF* from your Nikon and see it the way the camera will show it. Move a slider, the photo changes. Save the file when you like it.

* High Efficiency NEFs (HE and HE★) don't open directly yet. Convert them to DNG first, without JPEG XL compression.

Questions

Good to know.

Which cameras does it work with?

All EXPEED 7 cameras. The colour model was calibrated on a Nikon Z f; other bodies work, with small differences.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. The site only sends you the page itself; your NEF and JPEG files stay on your computer.

Is it free?

Yes, and the code is open (MIT licence). If it saves you an evening of editing, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi.

Will it match my edit exactly?

Close, not pixel-perfect. A picture control can only do what the camera can do: tones, colours and grading. Local edits, masks and retouching stay in your editor.

Is this a Nikon app?

No. It's an independent hobby project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nikon.

Made by one photographer, in the evenings.
