NP3 profile fitting

Assemble RAW + JPEG pairs, fit an NP3 profile, adjust it manually — all locally in the browser.

DISCLAIMER

  • The render is approximate: on average it differs from the real NX Studio render by dE ≈ 1.85.
  • The development is calibrated for the Nikon Z f; with other cameras the result is worse.
  • Frames are processed locally in the browser and are never sent anywhere.

Pair recommendations

  • The JPEG is made from the same RAW with one preset, without manual edits and local masks
  • The frame is not cropped or rotated, without strong vignetting and grain
  • Little overexposure in the frame: the white share in the target is below 30%
  • Active D-Lighting is left as shot
  • The more different colors the better: greys, skin, greenery, sky, saturated warm colors
  • More than 6 frames usually does not help the fit
